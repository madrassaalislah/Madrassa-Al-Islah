Our Academic System

Madrasa Al-Islah aims to provide a balanced education that combines Islamic knowledge with general education, English language development, and Arabic speaking skills.

Our Educational System & Facilities

Islamic Education

Islamic education is an essential part of our madrasa. We focus on Islamic knowledge, moral values, good character, and the teachings of Islam.

General Education

Alongside Islamic studies, our students receive general education so that they can gain knowledge and prepare themselves for future educational and career opportunities.

Special English Education

Special importance is given to English education. Skilled teachers guide students in developing their English knowledge, communication skills, and speaking confidence.

Arabic & English Speaking Practice

Our madrasa places special emphasis on speaking Arabic and English. Under the guidance of skilled teachers, students are encouraged to practise speaking both languages regularly.

Hostel Facilities

Hostel facilities are available for students. In the hostel, special attention is given to educational development, discipline, and regular Arabic and English speaking practice under the guidance of teachers.

Following the Sunnah

Special importance is given to following the Sunnah of our beloved Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him). Students are encouraged to practise the Sunnah in their daily lives, especially in the hostel environment.

Our Main Educational Objective

The main objective of Madrasa Al-Islah is to educate students in both Islamic studies and general education. We believe that students should gain strong Islamic knowledge while also developing their English language skills, Arabic communication abilities, and general academic knowledge. Our goal is to help students become knowledgeable, responsible, and successful individuals who can pursue higher education, build meaningful careers, and contribute positively to society while remaining connected to Islamic values and the Sunnah of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him).
