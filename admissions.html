Admissions at Madrasa Al-Islah

Welcome to Madrasa Al-Islah, where traditional Islamic education is combined with general academic learning, English-medium education, and Arabic and English language development.

Welcome to Our Admission Department

Madrasa Al-Islah provides an educational environment where students can pursue traditional Islamic studies alongside general education. Our aim is to prepare students for higher Islamic studies while developing their academic knowledge, language skills, discipline, and character.

Our Educational Departments

Alimiyyah Department

Madrasa Al-Islah offers traditional Islamic education through the Alimiyyah curriculum up to the Haftum (Seventh) level.

Students study the prescribed Islamic books and subjects, building a strong foundation for higher Islamic education and further studies in Dawrah-e-Hadith.

Hifz-ul-Quran Department

Our madrasa has a dedicated Hifz-ul-Quran department where students memorise the Holy Quran.

Alongside Quran memorisation, students also receive general education and academic guidance.

English-Medium General Education

General education is provided through the English medium from Class III to Class VIII.

Students study general academic subjects alongside the prescribed Islamic books and religious studies.

Arabic & English Language Development

Special attention is given to Arabic and English speaking skills.

Under the guidance of skilled teachers, students are encouraged to practise speaking both languages regularly.

Special Five-Year Programme for Hifz Students

Madrasa Al-Islah provides a special five-year educational programme for students who have memorised the Holy Quran.

The programme is designed to help Hifz students complete the prescribed Islamic curriculum up to the Haftum level within five years, alongside general academic education.

Special attention is given to developing their Islamic knowledge, academic understanding, and preparation for higher studies.

Preparation for Darul Uloom Deoband & Nadwatul Ulama, Lucknow

The curriculum at Madrasa Al-Islah is designed to prepare students for higher Islamic education after completing the Haftum level.

Students are guided and prepared to appear for admission examinations at renowned Islamic institutions such as Darul Uloom Deoband and Nadwatul Ulama, Lucknow.

The aim is to equip students with the knowledge and academic preparation needed to compete in entrance examinations and seek admission to advanced Islamic studies, including Dawrah-e-Hadith (the final advanced Hadith programme).

Admission to these institutions depends on their respective entrance examinations, eligibility requirements, and admission policies.

Our Educational Vision

The main objective of Madrasa Al-Islah is to provide students with a strong foundation in Islamic education alongside general academic learning.

We aim to develop knowledgeable, disciplined, and responsible students who can pursue higher Islamic studies, continue their academic development, and contribute positively to society while following Islamic values and the Sunnah of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him).
